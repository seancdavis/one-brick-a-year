# Candidate time events (how long ago)

One brick per year. Years are counted back from 2026. "About" means the number is rounded or the date is a range; anything without "about" is a known date. Each row has a card line written for an eight-year-old and, where it fits, a footer phrase ("before …"). Cut freely; the ones already on the stack are marked **(on stack)**.

Personal rows use the profile (age, and possibly two new fields).

## Mind-benders (pairs that surprise, for cards or the end screen)

- Cleopatra lived closer to the iPhone than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
- Mammoths were still alive when the Great Pyramid was built.
- Sharks are older than trees.
- Sharks are older than Saturn's rings (probably).
- T. rex lived closer to you than to Stegosaurus.
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire.
- Nintendo is older than the Eiffel Tower... by zero years. Same year.
- A shark alive today in Greenland may have been born when Shakespeare was alive.
- Bread is older than farming.
- The oldest living tree was a seed before the pyramids.
- For a billion years, nothing much happened, and scientists call it the boring billion.
- When the Moon was new, a day was six hours long.
