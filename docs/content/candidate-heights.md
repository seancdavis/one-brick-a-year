# Candidate physical comparisons (how tall)

The stack's height is real: one brick is 9.6 mm. Each row gives the thing, its height, how many bricks (years) the stack needs to reach it, a footer phrase, and an optional fun line for a card or the teaser. "About" marks a rounded or typical value. Rows already on the stack are marked **(on stack)**.

Heights are the honest measure. A few rows compare to a length (a bus, a whale) and say so in the phrase ("as long as").

## Personalization ideas

- Height: "as tall as you" needs her height. One more field on the start screen, or skip.
- Home: already there (one story, two stories, apartment).
- A landmark she knows: the tallest building in her city, or her school. Would need a name and a height typed in; probably not worth it.
