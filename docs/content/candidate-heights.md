# Candidate physical comparisons (how tall)

The stack's height is real: one brick is 9.6 mm. Each item gives the thing, its height, how many bricks (years) the stack needs to reach it, a footer phrase, and an optional fun line for a card or the teaser. "About" marks a rounded or typical value. Tick the box to keep an item, or delete its lines. Items already on the stack are ticked.

Heights are the honest measure. A few rows compare to a length (a bus, a whale) and say so in the phrase ("as long as").
